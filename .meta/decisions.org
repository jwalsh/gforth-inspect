Every decision that shapes how this repository works, in the order it was
made. *Human* is the owner. *Operator* is the coordinating agent session.
*Downstream* is the course session that consumes releases, relaying the
Human's decisions or proposing its own. An entry that replaces an earlier
one says so. The last column names what now enforces the decision: a file,
a script or a setting. A decision with nothing enforcing it is a wish.
