#+TITLE: Requirements discovery
#+STARTUP: showall

During requirements discovery:

1. Pin at SHA.
2. Log each run with an xrid.
3. Xref the call to its output by xrid.
4. Reflect in =.meta/=.

xrid: one id on the call (ref SHA, inspect.fs blob, engine, seed, cwd) and
on every record it produced (=log-id=).

Evidence, 2026-11-21: 25 decisions in one evening; =harden= moved six
times mid-run; every near-miss came from an unpinned ref.
