#+TITLE: 003 declared-effects: words.json against measured depth deltas
#+DATE: 2026-11-21
#+STARTUP: showall

* Hypothesis

Take every built-in word in the model (=git show model:model/data/words.json=,
2018 words) that has a declared data and/or float stack effect and is not
parsing, immediate, compile-only or a defining word, does no I/O, does not
throw on valid input and does not allocate. For each, give it inputs of the
declared types and run it under =catch=. The measured changes in =depth=
and =fdepth= then equal the declared effect.

Where an input is declared as an address, it is a valid, aligned address in
a scratch buffer, with room for whatever the word writes. Where it is
declared as =u= or =n=, it is small and in range.

* Prediction (written before the first run)

At least 95% of the tested words agree. Most disagreements come from the
model's parse of the effect comments (alternatives such as =( -- f | x f )=,
and the =w= / =x= / =d= cell counts), not from gforth.

* Gate

1. *Coverage.* Report how many words were tested, how many were excluded
   and why (one count per reason), and how many could not be typed.
2. *Agreement.* Held if at least 95% of the tested words agree on at least
   20 generated inputs each, in =gforth=. Repeat the disagreeing words in
   =gforth-fast= and =gforth-itc=.
3. *Classification.* Every disagreement is listed by word name and given
   one class: model parse, gforth differs from its own comment, harness
   typing, or a data-dependent effect (such as =?dup=).
4. *Calibration.* =pbt/calibrate/003-*.sh= edits one declared effect in a
   copy of words.json (for example, =dup= becomes =( x -- )=) and must go red
   on exactly that word.
5. *No crash is a pass.* A word that kills gforth (=SIGSEGV=, abort) is
   logged as excluded with its reason. It is never retried until it
   passes.

Out of scope: words that need a sandbox to run (file words, =system=,
=allocate=). Those get a count, not tests.

Markers (=...= =..= =|= =/= =??= =i*x= =j*x=) count 0 cells; =d= =ud= =xd=

*** Classification (gate 3): every disagreement has one class

/model parse (27)/
- alternatives summed as one list: =(BranchAddr?)= =CheckEnd=
  =MoreBranchAddr?= =(search-wordlist)= =search-wordlist= =find=
  =find-name= =sfind= =sfnumber= =snumber?= =environment?= =last?=
- variadic outputs: =(save-input)= =get-order= =save-see-flags= (=n*=)
- width only in the name, none in the model: =utime= (=dtime=),
  =cputime= (=duser dsystem=)
- synthesized =( -- w )= for end-struct names, which push two cells:
  =buffer%= =buffer-struct= =c-lib%= =c-prefix%= =cff%=
  =interpret/compile-struct= =list%= =wordlist-map-struct= =wordlist-struct=.
  Reproducer: =gforth -e 'buffer% depth . bye'= prints 2.
- the r* -> float heuristic on a Forth-source comment: =scratch ( r -- addr len )=
  takes no float. Reproducer: =gforth -e '1e scratch depth . fdepth . bye'= prints =2 1=.

/gforth differs from its own comment (1)/
- =uallot ( n -- )= leaves the old =udp= (=udp @ swap udp +!=, comp.fs:484).
  Reproducer: =gforth -e '1 uallot depth . bye'= prints 1.

/data-dependent effect (2)/
- =?dup=: =gforth -e '0 ?dup depth . clearstack 5 ?dup depth . bye'= prints =1 2=.
- =digit?= (=char -- digit true / false=): one or two cells by char.

/harness typing (0)/: none among the disagreements; but see the =<#= and
=assertn= notes below, both harness-state findings.

*** Things learned on the way

- The ci profile first failed: 200 calls of =hold= =xhold= =#= =#s= =<<#=
  without =<#= overflow the shared hold area and gforth exits (exit 2).
  The harness now runs =<#= before every call (commit 7e456e8). Shared
  interpreter state, not the stacks, is what leaks between calls.
- =assertn ( n -- )= with n above =assert-level= runs the compilation
  semantics of =(= and skips input to the next =)=, so it eats the next
  harness line without crashing. Excluded as parsing by name; a crash
  detector cannot see it.
- The model's call graph follows immediate words' compile-time behaviour
  (=IF= =?DO= =[char]=) as if it were run-time; the filter stops at
  immediate/compile-only callees and at =char=, otherwise 522 words looked
  like parsers.
- Agreement on alternatives is partly luck of typing: addresses point into
  zeroed memory, so =find= and friends always take the not-found branch.
  Those words show up as model parse here; with other inputs they would
  also be data-dependent.
